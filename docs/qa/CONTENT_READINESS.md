# Content readiness: what staff fill in before and after the release

Owner's order of 28 Oct (issue 259). The site ships with Sample content (every Sample record is marked and replaced
from the admin). This list is for the staff who replace it. Nothing here needs code or the server; every line is an
admin screen. Tick each line on the live site after the release.

| # | What | Where in Admin | Ready when | Done |

## 3. Never publish

- Trade licence numbers, owner details, ID numbers or fees.
- Anything for Hajj, Umrah, manpower or recruitment (Phase F).
- IATA, ATAB or TOAB badges the company does not hold.
- AI-generated, drawn or painted images; competitor photos.
- Restricted items in Waafa’s World.
- Invented reviews or testimonials.
