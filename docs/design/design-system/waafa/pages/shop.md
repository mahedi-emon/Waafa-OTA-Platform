# Waafas World overrides (WAAFA)

> Overrides `../MASTER.md` for `/shop` and its children. Source: PRD §10, HANDOFF route table (Shop*, ShopList*,
> ShopProduct*, ShopFinder*, ShopCart*, ShopCheckout*, ShopTrack*), DESIGN.md (cards), MOTION.md §8.

## Identity
- The header keeps the one WAAFA logo with "Waafas World" set as text beside it (heading font); no second mark.
  The phone tab bar's centre item is always labelled "Waafas World" (two lines allowed at 320 px) with Waafas World
  active inside the store.
- The store sells any category; no copy or layout may assume toner. Category filters come from each category's
  attribute set.

## Layout
- Store home: campaign carousel, category grid, collections, deals with end dates, best sellers, brands, new
  arrivals, service cards (Printing Solutions, International Trading), bulk banner, trust row; each row ordered and
  hidden from admin.
- Product cards: image, brand, title, price against crossed-out MRP with a labelled saving ("Save ৳…"), stock
  badge, Add → quantity stepper; "Choose options" for products with variants.
- Product page: gallery (+ optional real product video), variant swatches, quantity, Add to cart and Buy now,
  delivery estimate by zone, COD badge, highlights, specification groups (not a hairline table), warranty and
  returns, bulk quote, related and recently viewed.

## Motion
- Variant swatch ring morph, image cross-fade on variant change, add-to-cart arc with a badge pop.

## Commerce rules
- Prices in ৳ with lakh grouping, VAT included; cash on delivery up to the admin cap; offline payment with proof;
  online payment only when live; no restricted items.
