Thailand visa from Bangladesh: documents, fees and how long it takes

Most Bangladeshi travellers need a visa before flying to Thailand. The application is simple when the papers are in order; most refusals come from a weak bank statement or a missing employer letter. Here is what to prepare.

Who needs a visa

If you hold a Bangladeshi passport, you need a visa for tourism, business, treatment or study in Thailand. Children need their own visa, even when they travel on a parent’s booking.

Which visa to choose

Choose the visa that matches why you are going. A tourist visa covers holidays and visiting friends. Business trips, treatment and study each have their own visa with extra papers.

How long it takes

Plan for about two weeks from the day your papers are complete. Apply three to six weeks before you fly so there is time to fix anything the embassy asks for.

  1. Week 1Collect papers, we check them with you

  3. Week 3Decision and passport back

Mistakes that cause refusals

  • A bank statement with a large deposit just before applying, with no explanation.
  • An employer letter without a signature, seal or contact number.
  • Photos with a coloured background or glasses.
  • Hotel and flight dates that don’t match the dates on the form.

Questions

Can I apply without a job letter?
Yes, if you show other proof of ties to Bangladesh, such as business papers or property documents. We tell you what fits your case.
How much money should my bank statement show?
There is no fixed number, but it should cover your trip comfortably. We look at your statement and advise before you submit.
Can my family apply together?
Yes. Add everyone in one application. Children need their birth certificate and both parents’ consent.
