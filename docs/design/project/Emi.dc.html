How it works

Choose your trip or order

Send a request as usual. Tell your expert you want to pay by EMI, and for how many months.

Pay with your credit card

We take the full amount on your partner bank credit card at our office or by a secure payment link.

Your bank splits it

The bank turns it into monthly instalments on your card statement, usually within 7 working days.
