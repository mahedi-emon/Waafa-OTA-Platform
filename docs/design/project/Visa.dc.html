Questions

Good to know

Read our visa guides
Do you guarantee a visa?
No one can. The embassy decides. We make sure your file is complete and correct, which is the part that usually causes refusals.
Do I need to come to your office?
Not always. You can upload documents or send them on WhatsApp. Some embassies need you to visit their visa centre in person for fingerprints.
How do I pay?
Pay the embassy fee and our service charge by bank transfer, bKash, Nagad or at our Motijheel office. We give you a receipt for both.
Which visas do you handle?
Tourist, business, student, medical and transit visas.
