Istanbul and Cappadocia in a week

Three days in Istanbul’s old city and on the Bosphorus, then the valleys and cave hotels of Cappadocia. Flights from Dhaka, hotels, guides and your visa file, planned for you.

Duration7 days, 6 nights
FromDhaka, flights included
Group sizeUp to 16 travellers

Day by day

DAY1

Fly from Dhaka to Istanbul. Our partner meets you at the airport and takes you to your hotel in the old city. Evening free to rest.

FlightAirport transferHotel: old city, 4-star
DAY2

A guided walk through Sultanahmet: the Hippodrome, Hagia Sophia from outside and inside where open to visitors, Topkapı Palace and the Grand Bazaar. Lunch at a local lokanta.

BreakfastLunchGuide
DAY3

Morning cruise on the Bosphorus between Europe and Asia, then a free afternoon in Kadıköy or Taksim for food and shopping.

BreakfastCruise
DAY4

Short flight to Kayseri and transfer to a cave hotel in Göreme. Sunset at Love Valley if the weather is clear.

BreakfastDomestic flightCave hotel
DAY5

Early balloon flight if you add it (weather permitting, paid locally). Day tour of the Göreme Open-Air Museum, Uçhisar castle and a pottery workshop in Avanos.

BreakfastLunchGuide
DAY6

Morning flight to Istanbul, check in, and a free afternoon. Farewell dinner by the water.

BreakfastDomestic flightDinner
DAY7

Check out and transfer to the airport for your flight home.

BreakfastAirport transferFlight

What’s included

Included
  • Return flights Dhaka–Istanbul, economy, 30 kg checked bag
  • Two domestic flights Istanbul–Kayseri and back
  • Six nights in 4-star and cave hotels with breakfast
  • Airport transfers and day tours with an English-speaking guide
  • Lunch on days 2 and 5, farewell dinner on day 6
  • Visa file preparation and appointment booking
Not included
  • Türkiye visa fee paid to the embassy
  • Balloon flight (about €200 to €250, booked locally)
  • Meals not listed, drinks and tips
  • Travel insurance (we can add it)
