Video loops

Twelve silent loops made for the prototype, each with a poster frame. They play inline and muted, pause when off screen, and stay on the poster for people who prefer reduced motion. The route map, passport and product videos are motion graphics the site can use as they are. The scenery loops (clouds, lagoon, balloons, port, printer, city) are stand-ins: for the live site, swap in real footage with a free licence (Pexels or Coverr) at the same size, about 8 to 12 seconds, under 1 MB. Markup in the build: autoPlay muted loop playsInline poster.

Scene images in every photo slot

Prototype stand-ins: original drawn scenes, made for this prototype, fill every photo slot because the canvas can’t load external photos. The PRD rule stays for the live site: real photos only (Unsplash or Pexels until Waafa’s own arrive), never drawn or AI scenes. Each stand-in is mapped by its data-photo key, so the real photo replaces it without touching the layout. Credits for the planned photos are on the Photo brief.

Reduced motion and performance

Respect the system setting first. Animate only transform and opacity, and never block reading or tapping while something moves.

prefers-reduced-motion: reduceRise, slide and spring become a 150 ms fade. Marquee and Ken Burns stop. Counters show the final number.
Scroll reveals run onceIntersectionObserver with a 6% bottom margin; content is visible without JavaScript and in print.
BudgetsMotion loads lazily with LazyMotion and domAnimation (about 15 KB). No animation on the main thread longer than 16 ms; INP under 200 ms on a mid-range Android.
