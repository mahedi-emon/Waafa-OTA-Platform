Photo brief and credits

Every photo slot in the prototype carries its Unsplash image URL in data-src. Claude Code hotlinks these exact URLs at build time: w=1600 for heroes and large images, w=900 for cards.

32 supplied photos · 10 open slots
Prototype stand-ins, real photos in the build.The design canvas only shows files uploaded into it, and this workspace can’t reach images.unsplash.com. So every photo slot in the prototype now shows an original drawn scene as a stand-in, and a few panels play short rendered loops. The rule below still holds for the live site: real photos only, never drawn or AI scenes. In the downloadable prototype, run get-waafa-photos.mjs and the real photos listed here replace the stand-ins automatically.
Real photos onlyUnsplash License: free for commercial use. Replace with Waafa’s own photos over time. Never images from Google search.
Text on photosWhere text sits on a photo, add a soft navy gradient: transparent at 40%, navy-950 at 55% opacity at the bottom.
NeverAI-generated, painted or 3D-rendered images, or scenes drawn in CSS or SVG.
AvoidThe Kaaba, Masjid al-Haram, Madinah or any Hajj and Umrah imagery; airline logos on photos; recognisable celebrities.
