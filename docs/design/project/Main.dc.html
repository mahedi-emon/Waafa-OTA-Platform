Not yet, and the fix
  1. 1Photography. Theirs is real. Fix: your Unsplash photos are wired to every slot by URL and load at build time; the canvas shows labelled placeholders because it can’t load outside images.
  2. 2Proof of trust. They show offices, hotlines and payment partners. Fix: an honest trust layer on Home: office and directions, phone and WhatsApp, office hours, real payment methods, Facebook reviews instead of invented ones.
  3. 3Instant answers. Akij shows “from ৳” routes at once. Fix: group fares with fixed prices on Home, a plain line under Search saying what happens next, and (Stage D) matching group fares plus the reference number the moment a Manual query is sent.

What we learned, and what we left behind

Akij Air is the calmest of the four, so it set the bar for restraint. WAAFA takes the lessons, not the look: the destination finder, section labels, headings and copy are our own.

Akij Air

akijair.com
  • White header, few links, a headset “help” button instead of a loud hotline chip. Adopted, with live office hours inside the panel.
  • One rounded hero photo with the search card overlapping its lower edge; lots of white space.
  • Help band with opening hours above the footer.
  • “4.8 · Based on 50 reviews” with no source. WAAFA links to Facebook reviews instead.
  • The same red accent word in every heading. WAAFA keeps headings one colour and saves the accent for the hero.
  • List-plus-big-card destination layout. WAAFA uses its own destination finder with visa and flight-time filters.

ShareTrip

sharetrip.net
  • Student fare as a search option, not a separate page.
  • A clear grid of airlines, good for search engines and trust.
  • Ten product tabs in the search card, with a scroll arrow.
  • Advertising banners between sections.
  • Umrah fare type: not offered by WAAFA.

ShareTrip Shop

sharetrip.net/shop
  • Search bar overlapping the banner, then category tiles: the shop starts with finding, not browsing.
  • Discount tag plus MRP struck through; top brands with product counts.
  • Banners carry most of the message; no delivery or returns terms on the page.
  • Most cards have no add-to-cart.

GoZayaan

gozayaan.com
  • Short “how to book” videos in Bangla for first-time users.
  • Deals filtered by All, Flight, Hotel, Others, each with a coupon code.
  • Large partner advertisements inside the page.
  • Walls of card-network logos in the footer.

Adopt

10 patterns WAAFA takes

  1. 01

    One search card, four tabs

    Flight, Hotel, Tour and Visa share one glass card with a sliding indicator. The Search button is always in reach, and pinned inside every mobile picker.

    GoZayaanAkij Air
  2. 02

    City, code and airport together

    “Dhaka · DAC · Hazrat Shahjalal International”. Bangladeshi airports pinned first, then popular routes, with recent searches as one-tap chips.

    Akij AirGoogle Flights
  3. 03

    Return date on demand

    One-way is the default. “Add return” upgrades to round-trip in one tap, while the trip-type control stays visible for people who look for it.

    Akij Air
  4. 04

    Travellers and class in one sheet

    Steppers with the rules written next to them (infants no more than adults, nine travellers max), class as large radio cards, extra options folded away.

    Akij AirGoogle Flights
  5. 05

    Honest “from” prices

    Every fare before live booking reads “Indicative from ৳58,500 per adult”. Trending routes become admin-run group fares with seat counts the team actually enters.

    Akij AirObokash
  6. 06

    The itinerary on the card

    Package cards show nights per city (“3N Bangkok · 2N Pattaya”) and “from ৳ per person, twin sharing”, so people compare before they click.

    Obokash
  7. 07

    Visa by country, then by type

    Search, region and A–Z filters lead to a country page with a tab per visa type, a checklist, and fees split into embassy fee and service charge.

    ObokashShareTrip
  8. 08

    WhatsApp where it helps

    A floating button above the tab bar, prefilled with the page. After every query, a WhatsApp button prefilled with the reference number.

    Obokash
  9. 09

    A shop rhythm that sells

    Campaign, categories, best sellers, brands with counts, new arrivals. Waafas World adds Add to cart on every card, and Find by model wherever a category has compatibility data.

    ShareTrip Shop
  10. 10

    Results that explain themselves

    Sort tabs show the winning value (Cheapest ৳, Fastest 6h 05m). Filters are a rail on desktop and a sheet on mobile; the help card never scrolls away.

    SkyscannerBooking.com
Avoid

10 mistakes WAAFA won’t make

  1. 01

    Blocking pinch-zoom

    Two references ship user-scalable=no.

    Instead — zoom stays on; text scales to 200% without breaking (WCAG 1.4.4).

  2. 02

    Badge walls

    A dozen logos make every one of them mean less.

    Instead — only what Waafa really holds. No IATA badge until accredited; payment marks only for methods that are live.

  3. 03

    Prices you have to decode

    Two unlabeled numbers on one product card.

    Instead — price, crossed-out MRP and “save ৳” always labelled; travel fares marked indicative.

  4. 04

    Menu items that go nowhere

    Nav links pointing to “#”.

    Instead — every item in the header, More menu and footer has a real page on launch day.

  5. 05

    Fake urgency

    Countdowns and “only 2 left” with nothing behind them.

    Instead — seats left appear only when an admin entered them; no timers until live holds exist.

  6. 06

    Reviews nobody can check

    Big star averages with no source.

    Instead — approved feedback only, each with the service used and a date; no self-serving review markup.

  7. 07

    Search below the fold on phones

    Long hero copy pushes the button out of reach.

    Instead — the card starts in the first screen and its button sits in the thumb zone.

  8. 08

    One long form

    Asking for everything before saying anything back.

    Instead — two short steps, contact first, trip details prefilled from the search.

  9. 09

    Heroes with three jobs

    Offer sliders fighting the search card.

    Instead — the hero does one thing; offers start right after the trust strip.

  10. 10

    The template look

    Gradient blobs, emoji, icons in coloured circles, stock smiles.

    Instead — one icon family, one motif, one photo grade, and copy written for Bangladeshi travellers.

How WAAFA will look different
