Cabin bag or checked bag?

Power banks: cabin bag only

Up to 100 Wh is fine; 100 to 160 Wh needs the airline’s approval; over 160 Wh is not allowed. The Wh rating is on the label.

Cabin-safe power banks
Liquids: 100 ml each

On international flights, liquids in the cabin go in containers of 100 ml or less, all in one clear 1-litre bag.

Medicines with you

Keep medicines in the cabin with the prescription, especially for insulin or anything in liquid form.

Never: flammables

Lighter fluid, fireworks, gas canisters and strong chemicals are not allowed in any bag.

Never in checked bags: spare batteries

Spare lithium batteries, e-cigarettes and smart bags with fixed batteries must travel in the cabin.

Fragile and valuable

Keep jewellery, cash, documents and laptops in your cabin bag. Airlines limit what they pay for in checked bags.

Need more baggage?

Tell your expert when you book. Extra kilos added to the ticket cost less than paying at the airport.
