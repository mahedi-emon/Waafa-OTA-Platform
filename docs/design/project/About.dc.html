About us

Connecting the world, creating the future

WAAFA stands for Worldwide Alliance for Advancement, Future & Achievement. Waafa International began in 2010 serving Dhaka’s offices with printing and toner. In 2026 we opened Waafa Tours and Travel and Waafas World, our online store, with the same promise: people you can call, and an office you can visit.
