# Media credits

Every photo and video on the site is real: free-licence stock (Unsplash, Pexels, Coverr, Mixkit) until Waafa's own
arrive, or Waafa International's own product shots. Never AI-generated, painted or drawn (CLAUDE.md, correction 7).
